Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your operating hours?
Our main facility is open Monday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM, and Fridays from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Special weekend events will be announced on our community board.
Do I need to be a member to attend classes?
While we do host occasional public open houses, our regular weekly classes and resource library are exclusively for enrolled members to maintain a safe and consistent learning environment.
How can I schedule a tour of the facility?
You can schedule a tour by selecting "Membership Inquiry" in our contact form above. One of our coordinators will reach out within 24 hours to set up a convenient time for your family.
Are parents required to stay on-site during classes?
For children under 12, a parent or designated guardian must remain on the premises. We have a dedicated Parent Hub with fast Wi-Fi and coffee where you can work or relax!
Is there parking available?
Yes! We have a large, free dedicated parking lot located directly behind the building, with designated spots for drop-offs and pick-ups.
